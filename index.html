How to use it
  1. Click Share MapleStory window and pick the game window (or your whole screen).
  2. Open your inventory and hover each item with the grey bar at the top (those have hologram lines). Wait for the ✓ before moving on, about a second each.
  3. Hover one item of each slot so the scanner also sees what you're wearing (the "Currently Equipped" box).
  4. Don't move or sort items while scanning. When you're done, throw out what's marked Trash.

Stage 1 is the 18 lines: 3 Dimensional Leap, 3 White Hole, 3 Registry, 3 ATT & M.ATT, and one each of Crit DMG, Warrior, Mage, Bowman, Thief and Pirate, on six pieces (hat, top, bottom, cape, gloves, shoes). Stage 2 is a second set with the right sockets (4 with prismatic + stat + stat, 2 with stat + stat + stat); Orbs move your lines onto it.
