How to use it
  1. Click Share MapleStory window and pick the game window (or your whole screen).
  2. Open your inventory's Equip tab and expand it (the + at its top right) so all 16 columns show. Move the mouse off it for a moment: the Inventory map fills in, with dashed cells for items that have hologram lines. You can also paste a screenshot of the inventory window (Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V on this page).
  3. Hover each dashed item and wait for the ✓, about a second each. The map ticks the cell under your cursor and colours it keep, backup, donor or trash. Click a cell to see its lines and sockets.
  4. Hover one item of each slot so the scanner also sees what you have equipped (the "Currently Equipped" box).
  5. Don't move or sort items while scanning. When you're done, throw out what's marked Trash.

Tips for a clean scan

  • Run MapleStory windowed (or borderless), not exclusive fullscreen. If the share preview stays blank or spinning, that's why: switch to windowed, or share your Entire screen instead.
  • No live scan? Paste instead. Hover an item in the game, snip its tooltip (Win+Shift+S), then press Ctrl+V on this page. It's read just like a hover. Snip the inventory too if you want the map to tick its cell.
  • Show the game at full size. Tooltip text is tiny: pick the largest resolution that fits your screen and don't shrink the game window. Below about 90% size the text gets too blurry to read reliably (socket colours still work).
  • Use Chrome or Edge. Firefox's screen sharing often sends games black, frozen or half-drawn, so the scanner sees nothing. Pasting a screenshot of your inventory works in any browser.
  • Share the MapleStory window, not the browser. The browser can sit on top of the game (one monitor is fine), but don't minimise the game: a minimised window stops sending pictures.
  • On one monitor, the easiest setup is the game on one half and this page on the other (Windows key + ← / →).
  • Keep the inventory expanded (16 columns) and on plain background if you can; very bright scenery behind a tooltip makes it harder to read.
  • Hover one item at a time and wait for the ✓. Hover one item per slot first so your equipped gear is read.
  • Don't move or sort items mid-scan. If the map ever looks wrong, click Reset map.
