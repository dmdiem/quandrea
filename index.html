How to use it
  1. Click Share MapleStory window and pick the game window (or your whole screen).
  2. Open your inventory's Equip tab and move the mouse off it for a moment: the Inventory map fills in, with dashed cells for items that have hologram lines.
  3. Hover each dashed item and wait for the ✓, about a second each. The map ticks the cell under your cursor and colours it keep, backup, donor or trash. Click a cell to see its lines and sockets.
  4. Hover one item of each slot so the scanner also sees what you're wearing (the "Currently Equipped" box).
  5. Don't move or sort items while scanning. When you're done, throw out what's marked Trash.

Stage 1 is the 18 lines: 3 Dimensional Leap, 3 White Hole, 3 Registry, 3 ATT & M.ATT, and one each of Crit DMG, Warrior, Mage, Bowman, Thief and Pirate, on six pieces (hat, top, bottom, cape, gloves, shoes). Stage 2 is a second set with the right sockets (4 with prismatic + stat + stat, 2 with stat + stat + stat); Orbs move your lines onto it.
