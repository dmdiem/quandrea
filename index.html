How to use it
  1. Open the Equip tab, expanded to 16 columns, nothing hovered. Press PrtScn, then Ctrl+V here: the map fills in.
  2. Hover an item, press PrtScn, Ctrl+V here. Repeat for each dashed item.
  3. Throw out what says Trash.

Tips

  • Show the game at full size: tiny text reads badly.
  • Your equipped piece is read from the "Currently Equipped" box beside each tooltip.
  • Don't sort mid-scan. Map wrong? Reset map.
