Simpling Dumpling mode

Socket colours as big words, for colour-blind players.

Hover an item in the game. With OBS running its Virtual Camera, the colours show up live; or press PrtScn, then Ctrl+V here. ✓ = your class can use it.

Setting up OBS
  1. In OBS, add a Game Capture or Window Capture of MapleStory (the same as for streaming).
  2. Click Start Virtual Camera (bottom right in OBS).
  3. Here, click Use OBS camera and allow the camera. If another camera shows, pick OBS Virtual Camera in the list.

OBS only copies pictures of the screen, as when streaming; nothing touches the game, and nothing leaves your computer.

Inventory map optional

Open the Equip tab, expanded to 16 columns, with nothing hovered. Press PrtScn, then Ctrl+V on this page: the map shows which items have lines (dashed) and, once added, which to throw out (✕). Add a piece then goes cell by cell.
